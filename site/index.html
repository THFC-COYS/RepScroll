Your body is the only credential the feed cannot fake.

RepScroll locks your social apps behind movement. Push-ups, squats, or a plank, counted live on your iPhone. Do the reps, earn fifteen minutes. Skip the reps, stay locked out.

Passwords get saved. Face scans get spoofed by convenience. Screen time limits get tapped away by the same thumb that set them. RepScroll puts a small physical cost back at the door, at the exact second of intent, where habits actually live.

A gate, not a guilt report.

Most wellbeing tools report your failure after the scroll: a dashboard telling you that you wasted three hours yesterday. Useful shame, zero change. RepScroll intervenes before the scroll.

STEP 01

Pick your blocked apps

Choose the feeds that own your thumb. Instagram, TikTok, X, whichever ones you open on autopilot.

STEP 02

Drop and do the reps

Open a blocked app and the gate asks for proof of work: a set of push-ups, squats, or a plank hold, counted live by on-device pose detection.

STEP 03

Earn fifteen minutes

Finish the set and the apps unlock for fifteen minutes. When the window closes, the gate is back. Scrolling stops being the default and starts being a choice you paid for on purpose.

Three ways to pay the toll.

Every rep is counted by Apple's Vision framework, right on the phone. No wearables, no uploads, no honor system.

Vision · Body pose

Push-ups

Elbow-angle down-and-up transitions with a rep cooldown, so half reps and camera tricks do not count. Chest to the floor, earn the floor back.

Vision · Joint angles

Squats

Hip, knee, and ankle angles tracked through the full range of motion. Depth is detected, not declared.

Vision · Alignment

Plank

Shoulder, hip, and ankle alignment checked continuously. The timer only runs while your form holds. Sag, and the clock stops.

The camera proves you showed up. The proof stays yours.

RepScroll's pose engine runs VNDetectHumanBodyPoseRequest entirely on-device. Your body is the credential, and like any good credential, it is verified locally and never transmitted.

  • ✓ Camera frames are processed on the phone and never uploaded
